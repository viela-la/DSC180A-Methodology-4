**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic so far has been timbre, or the quality of a sound. Timbre determines how the same pitch played on two different instruments can still sound completely different.

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I'd want to investigate how to integrate motion-capture functionality into music-making software. Basically, I want to create music with my body movements.

**3. What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

With my domain's current approach, we're focusing more on vocals and speech, and the fundamentals of sound physics. For my potential investigation, I'd have to stray away from vocalization and more toward instrumentals and sound mixing.

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I'm really interested in the functionality of music-making software, like GarageBand, as well as motion-capture dancing video games that are able to "grade" the player based on their accuracy to the game's choreography, such as Just Dance and Dance Central. In Just Dance's case, they use a controller as an agent for motion-capture. But in Dance Central, they only use a camera -- so I'm especially interested in Dance Central's technique.
